## Ground rules

- **Observable behavior only.** Every rule says how a sentence gathers, turns, qualifies, lands, or sounds false.
- **No adjective-only guidance.** Do not write "warm", "professional", "smart", "authentic", or "friendly" unless you translate it into something a reader can see on the page. "Professional" is not a rule. "Gives the number before the explanation" is.
- **Every rule needs a sourced example:** a short excerpt from a real document, with the document and section. A rule without one is a guess. Leave it out.
- **Examples are evidence, not a phrase bank.** Never copy one into a draft.
- **Record only patterns that recur** across your samples, or that the writer confirmed. Leave out habits that belong to one publication or client.
